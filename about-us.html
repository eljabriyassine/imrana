About Imrana Rafat

Professional training. Academic foundations. A commitment to learner progress.

English language education, shaped around learners

With over 25 years of experience in English language education, I have taught learners across a wide range of age groups, proficiency levels and cultural backgrounds, from young learners and university students to adults, IELTS candidates and corporate professionals.

My experience spans IELTS preparation, General and Conversational English, and Professional Communication, working with learners from diverse cultural and linguistic backgrounds in school, university and workplace settings.

This breadth of experience has shaped my ability to adapt my teaching to individual needs and goals, whether learners are preparing for an international examination, pursuing academic ambitions or developing their English for personal and professional growth.
